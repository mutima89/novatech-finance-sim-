# NovaTech Solutions — Month-End Close Checklist

**Month:** June 2026
**Owner:** Finance Manager
**Target:** Accounts by 5th working day of following month

## Phase 1: Pre-Close (Day -3 to Day 0)
- [ ] Cut-off reminders sent to all departments
- [ ] All expense reports submitted for current month
- [ ] All sales orders processed through system
- [ ] All purchase orders approved and coded
- [ ] Review open purchase orders for accruals needed

## Phase 2: Bank & Cash (Day 1)
- [ ] Barclays Main account reconciled
- [ ] Barclays Payroll account reconciled
- [ ] Starling Expense card account reconciled
- [ ] Petty cash counted and reconciled
- [ ] All bank fees and interest posted
- [ ] Foreign currency revaluation (if applicable)
- [ ] **→ Output:** Completed bank rec with outstanding items list

## Phase 3: Revenue & Receivables (Day 1-2)
- [ ] All sales invoices generated for month
- [ ] Credit notes issued (if any)
- [ ] Deferred revenue schedule reviewed
- [ ] Deferred revenue amortised correctly
- [ ] Aged debtors run and reviewed
- [ ] Bad debt provision calculated (if needed)
- [ ] Cash receipts allocated to invoices
- [ ] **→ Output:** Revenue reconciliation, aged debtors report

## Phase 4: Payables & Accruals (Day 2)
- [ ] All supplier invoices entered and coded
- [ ] Prepaid expenses amortised
- [ ] Accruals for unbilled items booked
- [ ] Intercompany reconciliations (if applicable)
- [ ] Credit card statements reconciled
- [ ] **→ Output:** AP aged report, accruals schedule

## Phase 5: Payroll & People (Day 2-3)
- [ ] Payroll journal posted
- [ ] Employer NI accrual checked
- [ ] Pension contributions reconciled
- [ ] PAYE/NI liability balanced to HMRC account
- [ ] Employee benefit accruals (holiday, commission)
- [ ] **→ Output:** Payroll reconciliation

## Phase 6: Fixed Assets (Day 3)
- [ ] Depreciation charge calculated and posted
- [ ] Amortisation of intangibles calculated and posted
- [ ] Asset additions verified (purchase orders matched)
- [ ] Asset disposals processed (if any)
- [ ] **→ Output:** Fixed asset register updated

## Phase 7: Tax & Compliance (Day 3-4)
- [ ] VAT return prepared and reviewed
- [ ] VAT control account reconciled to return
- [ ] CIS/RTI submissions confirmed
- [ ] Corporation tax accrual reviewed
- [ ] **→ Output:** VAT return, tax packs

## Phase 8: Management Accounts (Day 4)
- [ ] Trial balance reviewed (sign-offs)
- [ ] P&L vs Budget analysis
- [ ] Balance sheet reviewed (all accounts reconciled)
- [ ] Cash flow statement prepared
- [ ] KPIs and commentary drafted
- [ ] **→ Output:** Management accounts pack

## Phase 9: Board Pack (Day 5)
- [ ] Executive summary written
- [ ] Variance commentary completed
- [ ] Forecast updated
- [ ] Board pack compiled and reviewed
- [ ] **→ Output:** Board pack submitted

## Phase 10: Post-Close (Day 5+)
- [ ] Audit files updated
- [ ] Journal entries filed and numbered
- [ ] Software subscriptions reconciled
- [ ] Sales commission calculated
- [ ] Month-end file saved to shared drive
