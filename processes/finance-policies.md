# NovaTech Solutions Ltd. — Finance Policies & Procedures

## 1. Purchase Order Policy
- All purchases over £250 require a purchase order
- PO must be approved by budget holder before ordering
- AP will not process invoices without matching PO
- Three-way match: PO → Goods Received → Invoice

## 2. Expense Reimbursement
- Employees submit via expense system by month-end
- Receipts required for all expenses over £10
- Client entertainment over £50 per head requires pre-approval
- Mileage: 45p/mile (HMRC approved rate)
- Overseas travel needs CEO approval

## 3. Credit Control Policy
| Aging | Action | Owner |
|-------|--------|-------|
| 0-30 days | Statement sent | Finance |
| 31-45 days | Email reminder + phone call | Finance |
| 46-60 days | Formal letter + call to Director | Finance + Sales |
| 61-90 days | Final demand, stop service | Finance Manager |
| 90+ days | Escalate to collections / legal | CEO |

## 4. Delegated Authority Limits
| Role | Capex | Opex | Contract Value |
|------|-------|------|---------------|
| Finance Manager | £2,500 | £5,000 | £10,000/yr |
| Sales Director | — | £2,500 | £25,000/yr |
| CTO | £5,000 | £5,000 | £15,000/yr |
| CEO | £25,000 | £25,000 | £50,000/yr |
| Board | >£25,000 | >£25,000 | >£50,000/yr |

## 5. Revenue Recognition Policy
- SaaS subscriptions: Recognised ratably over subscription term
- Professional services: Recognised upon delivery / milestone
- Annual contracts: Revenue deferred and recognised monthly
- Refunds: Provision at 2% of gross revenue

## 6. Capitalisation Policy
- Software development: Capitalise after technical feasibility established (GAAP: ASC 350-40)
- Computer equipment: Capitalise if >£500 and >1 year useful life
- Office equipment: Capitalise if >£1,000 and >2 years useful life
- All capitalised assets depreciated straight-line

## 7. VAT Policy
- Standard-rated supplies (20%): All SaaS subscriptions to UK customers
- Zero-rated: Exports to non-UK businesses (reverse charge)
- Input VAT reclaimed on all business expenses
- Quarterly returns filed (Mar/Jun/Sep/Dec)
- Payment due 30 days after quarter end

## 8. Foreign Currency
- Functional currency: GBP
- Transactions in foreign currency recorded at spot rate
- Monetary assets/liabilities revalued at month-end
- Exchange gains/losses taken to P&L
