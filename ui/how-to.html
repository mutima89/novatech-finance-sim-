1 What Is This?

NovaTech Solutions Ltd. is a made-up SaaS company in Manchester with 48 employees. You are the Finance Manager. Every day you get tasks that a real finance person would do — checking bank statements, chasing customers who haven't paid, calculating payroll, preparing reports for the board, and dealing with auditors.

There are 10 days of work split into 2 weeks. Each day has 3-4 tasks. You work through them at your own pace. There are no right or wrong answers — the point is to learn by doing.

💡 Who is this for? Beginner finance students, people learning accounting, anyone who wants to see what a real finance job looks like without the pressure. You don't need to be an expert — the tasks teach you as you go.

6 The Finance Tools (Calculators)

Four tools are built right into the interface. Click 🔧 Finance Tools in the sidebar.

💰 Bank Reconciliation

What it does: You type in your General Ledger entries (one per line in the format Date | Ref | Description | Amount) and it totals them up. Compare the total to your bank statement to check if everything matches.

When to use: Day 1 task — reconciling Barclays Main account.

🧾 VAT Control Account

What it does: Enter your opening VAT balance, total output VAT from sales, and total input VAT from purchases. It calculates whether you owe HMRC money or can reclaim some.

When to use: Day 1 (VAT check) and Day 7 (quarterly VAT return prep).

📐 How VAT works in the UK (simplified):
• Output VAT = 20% of what you charge customers
• Input VAT = 20% of what your suppliers charge you
• You pay HMRC the difference: Output − Input
• If Input > Output, HMRC owes you money

📈 Cash Flow Forecast

What it does: Enter your opening balance plus expected weekly inflows and outflows. It projects your cash position week by week and warns you if cash runs low.

When to use: Day 3 when Sarah asks for a July cash flow forecast.

📊 Depreciation Calculator

What it does: Enter an asset's cost, residual value, and useful life. It calculates annual and monthly straight-line depreciation.

When to use: Day 4 and Day 8 when preparing the fixed asset register and posting depreciation journals.

10 Tips for Each Day

Day 1 — Bank Reconciliation

Compare the GL entries listed in your task to the bank statement. One customer (Oxbridge Analytics) says they paid — check if the money actually arrived. If not, that's a discrepancy to investigate. For the VAT check, use the VAT Calculator tool — add up output VAT from all sales invoices and input VAT from all purchase invoices.

Day 2 — Credit Control

Sort the aged debtors by how overdue they are. The oldest ones (Sterling Partners at £12,500 from March) need the most urgent action. For the credit check on Meridian Trust, think about their credit score (62/100), the CCJ on record, and whether you'd trust them with a £2,499/mo contract.

Day 3 — Cash Flow Forecast

Use the Cash Flow tool. Start with the current balance (£159,361.50). Estimate weekly inflows (collections from customers) and outflows (payroll, suppliers, loan repayment). The tool will show you if cash runs low at any point.

Day 4 — Management Accounts

The P&L template is in tools/pl-statement-template.csv. Fill in the May actual vs budget numbers. The biggest variance tells you where to focus. For the fixed asset register, calculate monthly depreciation: cost ÷ (useful life in years × 12).

Day 5 — Board Pack

This is where you present everything you've done. The board will ask tough questions — prepare answers. For the chargeback, check if the customer who filed it (R. Davies) matches your customer WebCraft Agency (INV-2026-0614). If yes, you can prove service was delivered.

Week 2 (Days 6-10) — Month-End Close

This is the "real work" of finance. You're closing the month — finalising all revenue, booking accruals, processing payroll, producing the balance sheet and cash flow statement, and presenting to the board with a strategic plan. Use all the tools and data you've learned from Week 1.

12 Common Questions

Do I need to know accounting?

No. The tasks explain everything as you go. If you see a term you don't recognise (like "accrual" or "deferred revenue"), the task brief explains it. The finance policies document (processes/finance-policies.md) also explains how NovaTech handles each accounting area.

Is there a "correct" answer?

For some tasks yes (e.g. "does the bank reconciliation balance?"). For others, it's about judgement (e.g. "should we accept this settlement?"). The point is to think through the decision and defend your reasoning.

Where do I save my work?

The output/ folder is for your work. Save spreadsheets, notes, or decisions there. The Work Tracker in the HTML interface saves automatically.

I finished both weeks — what now?

You've completed 10 days of real-world finance tasks. You've done: bank recs, credit control, cash flow forecasting, management accounts, board reporting, month-end close, audit prep, and strategic planning. That's pretty much everything a Finance Manager does in a month. Try running through again and making different decisions to see how things play out differently.

📌 Remember: This is a simulation. The company, people, and numbers are all fictional. But the tasks are based on real finance jobs at real SaaS companies. What you learn here transfers directly to the real world.
