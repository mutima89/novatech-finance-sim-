📅
A Day in Finance Monday morning
What a finance manager actually does
Ever wondered what a Finance Manager does on a Monday morning? Here's a real example from the simulation: ☕ 9:00 — Open email. Sarah (CEO) wants May accounts by Friday. Also, a customer claims they paid but we can't see it. 📊 9:30 — Start bank reconciliation. Match 16 GL entries to the bank statement. Find one doesn't match. 📧 10:30 — Email Sarah: "Oxbridge didn't pay. Let me call them." 🧾 11:00 — VAT check. Opening balance £12,300. Output VAT from 20 sales invoices minus input VAT from 18 purchase invoices. 📝 12:00 — Calculate closing VAT position. ✅ 14:00 — Done. Move through the tracker. That's Day 1. Days 2-10 get progressively deeper: credit control, payroll, cash flow forecasting, full month-end close, board presentation. If you can do all 10 days, you've basically done a finance internship. #Finance #DayInTheLife #Accounting #LearnByDoing #Simulation

Stack
⚙️
Built With Tech stack
How it's built and why
This simulation has zero dependencies. Here's the stack: Frontend (ui/index.html): • Vanilla HTML — no frameworks • Vanilla CSS — no Tailwind, no Bootstrap • Vanilla JavaScript — no React, no Vue, no npm • All data embedded as JS objects — no API calls, no database • localStorage for progress persistence Backend (optional): • PowerShell scripts for bank rec, VAT, cash flow • Git for version control Total: 46 files, 3,600+ lines, 0 dependencies. Why build it this way? • Anyone can open index.html and it works — no install, no server • Students shouldn't need to configure anything • No internet required — it's all in the file • Easy to fork, modify, and extend • Teaches the finance, not the tech The simulation is what matters — the tech just gets out of the way. #WebDev #VanillaJS #OpenSource #EdTech #BuildInPublic

Week 1 vs Week 2

Two Weeks, Two Levels Progressive difficulty
How the simulation builds your skills
The simulation is split into 2 weeks — and they're very different: Week 1 — Mid-Month (Days 1-5) You learn the core finance workflows: • Bank reconciliation • VAT control • Aged debtors & credit control • Cash flow forecasting • P&L vs budget analysis • Board reporting • Chargeback investigation Week 2 — Month-End Close (Days 6-10) You close the books and produce full accounts: • Revenue reconciliation & journals • Supplier negotiation & accruals • Payroll processing with new hires • Depreciation & amortisation • Full trial balance • Balance sheet • Cash flow statement • H2 reforecast & strategic planning Week 1 teaches you the pieces. Week 2 shows you how they fit together. You get the full picture by Day 10. #Finance #Learning #MonthEndClose #Accounting #ManagementAccounts
